🔒 Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection: KCAS Attendance collects faculty name, staff ID, department, registered profile photographs, and live selfie captures exclusively for institutional attendance verification.

2. Location Services: GPS coordinates are captured at check-in/out to verify presence within the college 100-meter campus radius. Real-time background tracking is never performed.

3. Biometric Verification: Facial features are compared client-side and verified against your administrative profile photo. Biometric data is never shared with third parties.

4. Teaching Records: Blackboard photographs and teaching notes are archived securely for academic accreditation and duty auditing.

5. Retention & Security: Passwords and sessions use cryptographically secured hashing and token authentication.

📑 Terms & Conditions

1. Institutional Use: This application is the official property of Krishna College of Arts and Science, Karur. Access is restricted to authorized faculty staff, HODs, and administrators.

2. Attendance Integrity: Staff must physically mark attendance on campus. Any attempt to spoof GPS location or use unauthorized photographs constitutes academic misconduct.

3. Class Duty Compliance: Faculty members are expected to record lesson topics and photo proofs for all designated class periods daily.

4. Leave Protocol: Leaves must be submitted in advance and are subject to approval by the respective Head of Department or Principal.
